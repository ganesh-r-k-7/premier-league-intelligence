## Known Limitations (from Implementation Contract)

1. **Single season only (2015/16):** No cross-season validation possible.
2. **Matchweek = simultaneous assumption:** Matches within same matchweek treated as concurrent.
3. **No opponent/team strength features:** Baselines are purely historical averages.
4. **Position from modal event, not lineup role:** Affects position_team_median estimation.
5. **Stoppage time in minutes:** Match-end minutes include stoppage time (91-101).
6. **Unused substitutes in Population A:** 0-minute players receive predictions but actuals=0.
7. **No injury/suspension/rotation signal:** Expected minutes use historical medians only.
8. **Penalty goals included in goals:** Baselines predict total goals.
9. **One 0-minute substitute:** Jordi Gómez (Sunderland) appears in Population A with 0 minutes.
